Reading Time: 2 minutes Ads Manager runs two campaign types. Standard campaigns work the way you’d expect, with a headline, description, image, and landing page you build yourself. Product feed campaigns are sponsored product placements that appear beneath ChatGPT’s responses, generated directly from a brand’s product feed.
